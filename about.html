Harry and Frances Doppelganger, and their daughters Emma and Faith, are dedicated to hosting guests at their Bed and Breakfasts.

They own four Bed and Breakfasts located in Truckee Lake Tahoe, California, Put-In-Bay, Ohio, Erie, Pennsylvania, and Conneaut Lake, Ohio.

They specialize in small town experiences and hospitality in the Midwest, West and the Great Lakes region.

Mountain Oasis Bed and Breakfast is located in Lake Tahoe Truckee in the Sierra Nevada Mountains. This quaint town is the birthplace of downhill skiing and home to the 1960 Winter Olympics. The name Truckee was derived from the name Tru-ki-zo, a Paiute chief, who engaged in commerce with settlers to the region. Truckee started out in 1863 on the wagon road crossing the Sierras. It became a stop on the Central Pacific Railroad in 1868. Truckee is a town with a population of nearly 17,000 incorporated in Nevada County, California.

Near Lake Taho, it is a great place for mountain climbing, cycling, hiking, shopping, eating at restaurants and skiing. In the Fall there are wine festivals, music festivals and Octoberfest. Fall colors make a drive through the region spectacular. Activities on the lake include boating and swimming. Truckee is a perfect place for solo travelers, couples, groups and families. The weather is typically warm and dry in the summer, and snowy and cold in the winter. The town is located at an elevation of 6,000 feet. Sudden weather changes are not uncommon. Truckee receives about 200 inches of snow or more annually. The town features many bars, small batch breweries and restaurants for every taste. There are shopping malls and stores to buy outdoor gear. Shops downtown include stores for gourmet foods, clothing for children, gifts and jewelry. There are also many spas and wellness centers.
