Sierra Nevada Mountains


Perfect for weekend getaways or for longer stays, guests enjoy homestyle breakfasts, skiing, swimming, hiking and outdoor activities. Great value for money, you will not find a better deal anywhere in Lake Tahoe. Mountain Oasis Bed and Breakfast features rooms with queen beds and their own bathrooms, an accessible room and bathroom and family suite comprising two rooms with a shared bathroom.

Mountain Oasis Bed and Breakfast is the newest of a four family-run bed and breakfasts located in Ohio, Pennsylvania and California. Truckee Lake Tahoe is a popular tourist resort town and rooms fill up quickly. Get started by booking a room now. Services include a full breakfast each day, spa bookings and ski bookings. The views of mountains, forests and lakes from the resort are stunning. The alpine air is refreshing and invigorating. It is the perfect place for a quiet weekend or a busy family outing. You can buy all the outdoor gear you need at the malls in town. Our prices are very competitive. Book now.
