Spa packages are a favorite for our weekend guests, and we will arrange the for you even on short notice. Your booking may be made in advance on the reservation form.

We will arrage your ski package for a day of skiing. The package includes ski lift, ski rental and ski lessons with a local pro. We will also provide drinks and snacks for your day trip to the ski resort. Transportation can be arranged upon request.

Swimming and watersports on the lake are at your own discretion. Watersports rental shops are open during the summer months along the lake.

Hiking trails are open all year round and are a favorite during the Fall. We provide maps and a local guide may be hired. You may request drinks and snacks for your day of hiking.

For a more laid back experience, we recommend dining in Truckee at the many restaurants and shopping in the local boutiques. Our many guests have left their own recommendations in our guestbook.
